概要 Festival Lore

Where Tradition Meets Innovation

Named after the timeless quest for wisdom and elevated by the serenity of Chureito beneath Mount Fuji.

🌸

Crafting Technology with Ancient Mastery

In classical Japan, master artisans devoted lifetimes to the pursuit of absolute perfection—a philosophy known as Shokunin Kishitsu. At ANVESHAN 3.0, we channel that timeless devotion into the vanguard of modern computing, artificial intelligence, and mechanical engineering.

On October 9 – 10, 2026, thousands of creators, coders, and scholars gather from across the nation to share breakthroughs, forge solutions, and honor the sacred intersection of art and intellect.
